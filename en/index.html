    Common questions

    Can I see a car before I decide?

    Yes. Every car is on the showroom floor. Book a visit and we have it ready for you.

    How do I get the price?

    Press Ask about this car on any listing. It opens WhatsApp with the car already named, and we reply with the price.

    Where do the cars come from?

    Each listing shows the country the car was imported from, so you know its origin before you visit.

    Can I reserve a car?

    Message us about the car you want. Reserved cars are marked on the listing so you know their status.

    Can I trade in my current car?

    Ask when you visit. Bring the car and the papers and we will look at it with you.

    What are the opening hours?

    Hours are listed in the contact section below.
